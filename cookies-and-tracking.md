# Cookies & Tracking Policy

_Last updated: September 12, 2026_

1. **What this policy covers.** It explains what information the App stores or records in relation to the buyer's and the merchant's browser, and how consent is managed.
2. **We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking.** The App does not set advertising cookies, does not build profiles for marketing purposes, and does not track people across other sites.
3. **What we do use.**

   | Technology | Category | Purpose | Data |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | The App's web pixel | Analytics / attribution | Record that a purchase was paid by transfer, for the merchant's dashboard | Order ID, amount, currency, codes — no buyer data |
   | Session token | Strictly necessary | Securely operate the App's blocks (upload/approve receipt) | Shopify technical session token |
   | App preference | Functional | Remember display settings | No personal data |

4. **Consent.** Where the law requires it (for example, in the EU and the United Kingdom), the web pixel's attribution recording must be conditioned on the consent state the buyer expresses in the merchant's banner, read through Shopify's Customer Privacy API. The App does not require consent for strictly necessary technologies.
5. **Banner responsibility.** The storefront consent banner is managed by the merchant as controller. Rubaru documents its pixel here and respects the consent signal.
6. **California residents.** We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA and we respect the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where applicable.
7. **How to control it.** You can manage consent from the merchant's banner and from your browser settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect the validity of prior processing.
8. **Contact.** support@rubaru.tech.
